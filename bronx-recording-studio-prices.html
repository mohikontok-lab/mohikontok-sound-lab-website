Frequently asked questions

How much does a recording studio cost in the Bronx?

At Mohikontok Sound Lab, Room A is $65 per hour and Room B is $55 per hour, with a minimum session of 2 hours — so a first session starts at $130 for Room A or $110 for Room B, before any first-visit discount.

Do you charge per hour or per song?

Studio time is charged per hour. Mixing and mastering are priced per song, from $250 and $75 respectively. Packages run $199–$699.

Is there a discount for a first visit?

Yes — 25% off your first visit to the studio. Tick the "First visit to the studio — 25% off" box on the booking form and the discounted total is calculated for you.

How far ahead do I need to book?

At least 24 hours ahead. The studio is open 24/7 by booking.

Where is the studio?

1389 Kearney Ave, Bronx, NY 10465 (Country Club area). Phone (347) 497-3589, email studio@mohikontok.com.
