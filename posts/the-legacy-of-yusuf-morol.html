The Legacy of Yusuf Morol

November 6, 2026

More than half a century ago, in a quiet riverside village in Bangladesh, lived a gifted songwriter and singer named Yusuf Morol.

He wrote of love, devotion, and the unvarnished grace of everyday life. His melodies drifted through village fairs, riverbanks, and tin-roof tea stalls — carried entirely by word of mouth, shared heart to heart long before digital networks existed.

Yet Yusuf never had the tools or infrastructure to safeguard what he created. There were no ISRC codes, no metadata registries, no legal anchors — only handwritten notebooks yellowing at the edges, and magnetic cassette tapes gradually demagnetizing with time.

Decades later, when his descendants sought to revive his catalog and bring his life’s work into the modern era, they ran straight into an unforgiving barrier: without verifiable registration and chain of title, they could neither claim legal ownership nor secure his rightful royalties. The songs were alive in the collective memory, but legally, his ownership had dissolved into thin air.

From Loss to Purpose

Witnessing the quiet erasure of a family legacy catalyzed a turning point. Decades after Yusuf sang by the river waters, his great-grandson transformed that unresolved loss into a structural mission: Mohikontok.

Named after the concept of a “two-way river” — where artistic creation flows outward to the world and recognition, rights, and revenue flow back to the creator — Mohikontok was established to shield independent artists from the exact fate Yusuf met.

Through our Global Rights Distributor (GRD) framework, we provide the vital bridge between grassroots creativity and worldwide commercial infrastructure.

The Mission

At Mohikontok, we believe every melody, lyric, spoken recording, and visual story requires an indelible digital fingerprint — immutable proof of authorship engineered to outlast generations.

No creator’s life work should be swallowed by time, legal obscurity, or bureaucratic inertia. We equip independent musicians, authors, and audio engineers with verified ISRC and UPC tracking, metadata protection, and worldwide catalog registration. We handle the technical and administrative barriers so your work remains unambiguously yours — across digital stores, international collection societies, and every platform that plays your sound.

A Word from the Founder

“My great-grandfather’s voice still echoes through our culture, but the global registries have no record of his name. I built Mohikontok and the Global Rights Distributor framework so that no creator — whether recording in a rural village or a city sound lab — is ever silenced or uncredited again. Your art is your legacy. We ensure the world remembers who built it.”

— Mohammad Nasirullah, Founder, Mohikontok Sound Lab & Global Rights Distributor
