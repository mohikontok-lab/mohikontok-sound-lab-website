How Much Does a Recording Studio Cost in the Bronx?

November 4, 2026

The first question almost every artist asks before booking is also the hardest to answer from a Google search: studio websites often hide their rates, or quote a single number that turns out to mean something different once mixing, mastering, and minimums are added. Here is the honest breakdown — what a session costs, what each part of the bill is for, and what it costs at our studio in particular.

The short answer

Most Bronx studios charge by the hour for room time, and per song for mixing and mastering. At Mohikontok Sound Lab, Room A is $65 per hour and Room B is $65 per hour, with a minimum session of 2 hours — so a first session starts at $130 for either room, before any discount. Mixing starts at $250 per song and mastering at $75 per song. Packages run $199–$699.

What the hourly rate buys

The hourly rate is room time in a treated space: the live room or the vocal suite, the microphones and monitoring in it, and a room that is quiet enough for a clean take. It is not the finished song. Think of it like renting a kitchen: the hour buys the room and the tools, and the cooking — mixing and mastering — is priced separately.

Why mixing and mastering are per song

A mix is not measured in hours at the desk so much as in decisions per track: balancing vocals against instruments, cleaning noise, shaping space, and making the song translate from headphones to a car stereo. That work is priced per song because every song is a different amount of work. Mastering, the final polish that makes a track consistent and ready for release, is likewise per song.

The three costs people forget

Minimums. Many studios, including ours, require a minimum session — ours is 2 hours. A "cheap" hourly rate with a 4-hour minimum can cost more than a higher rate with a 2-hour one. Always multiply rate by minimum before comparing.

Musicians. If you need session players, that is usually extra. At the Lab, in-house musicians are +$30 per hour.

The first visit. This is the one discount worth asking about everywhere: our first visits get 25% off, applied on the booking form. A $130 Room A session becomes $97.50 on a first visit.

How to compare studios fairly

Ask every studio the same four questions before you book: What is the hourly rate, and what is the minimum? Is mixing included or per song? Is mastering included or per song? Is there a first-visit or package price? The studio that answers all four plainly is usually the one that will bill you plainly, too.
