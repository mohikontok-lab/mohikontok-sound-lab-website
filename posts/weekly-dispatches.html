The Weekly Dispatch, from the first issue

November 2, 2026

Every week, Mohikontok Sound Lab sends a Weekly Dispatch: one studio craft feature, one LISTEN from the Lab, and one WATCH from a session. Here is how the series has run, from the first issue.

Week of October 5 — the first issue: events

The series opened with events: live musicians for hire and full sound systems for hire — vocalists, instrumentalists, DJs, and ensembles, plus professional gear set up and run by people who do this for a living — for birthdays, weddings, community gatherings, and corporate events. One call covers the talent and the tech.

Week of October 12 — show up session-ready

The feature was a studio habit that saves money and makes records sound better: show up session-ready. Do a session dry run 48 hours before, put everything in one folder — your instrumental as a 24-bit WAV labeled with song title and BPM, your lyrics as a clean sheet — and warm up 10 minutes before you leave the house.

🎵 LISTEN: “Paap” — Golam Mostofa performing Kazi Nazrul Islam, recorded at the Poetry Camp and released October 6.

🎬 WATCH: a 4-second tour of the live room under purple LED lighting — the vocal mic on its stand, guitars along the wall, and the laptop DAW station ready to roll.

Week of October 19 — mic technique

The feature was the skill that separates good vocal recordings from great ones: mic technique. Home base is a hand's width — about four to six inches — from the mic; lean in on the quiet lines, ease back on the big chorus notes, and angle the mic about 15 degrees so plosives sail past while your tone stays full.

🎵 LISTEN: “Shoyeb Monir feat. Lalon Sai” — a Lalon Shah recording cut right here in the studio.

🎬 WATCH: six minutes of a vocal take in progress — mic in hand, lyrics on the phone, the real work of getting a song down.

Week of October 26 — the Halloween issue

The feature was clean voice audio for reels, shorts, and podcasts: your phone camera is good enough, but your phone microphone in a noisy room usually isn't — and the pro move is recording your voice and your video separately, then syncing them. The issue carried a Halloween treat — 15% off a first visit — and welcomed 15 Bronx arts organizations as new readers. It was sent October 26 to 264 contacts.

🎵 LISTEN: Franz Kafka's The Metamorphosis, dubbed in Bengali, posted October 23.

🎬 WATCH: a folk song performed by Shoab Monir.
