Rehearsal Space in the Bronx

Book a room by the hour for band rehearsal, run-throughs, and practice at Mohikontok Sound Lab, 1389 Kearney Ave, Bronx, NY 10465 — in the Country Club area, serving artists from across the Bronx, including Throggs Neck, Parkchester, Morris Park, and Pelham Bay.

Book rehearsal time

Who rehearses here

Bands working up a set, vocalists running through songs, groups preparing for a show or a recording session, and spoken-word and poetry performers rehearsing with live sound. If you need a treated room and a quiet place to hear yourselves properly, this is that room.

Frequently asked questions

Can a band rehearse here?

Yes. Book Room A for full-ensemble rehearsal at $65 per hour, or Room B for smaller groups at $55 per hour. Sessions are a minimum of 2 hours.

Do you provide musicians for rehearsals?

Yes — in-house musicians at +$30 per hour, including HandSonic HPD-20, MX49, AeroBand, and chimes. Tick the session-musician box on the booking form.

How far ahead do I need to book?

At least 24 hours ahead. The studio is open 24/7 by booking.

Where is the studio?

1389 Kearney Ave, Bronx, NY 10465 (Country Club area). Phone (347) 497-3589, email studio@mohikontok.com.
