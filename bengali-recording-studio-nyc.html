Bengali Recording Studio in New York

A Bronx studio for Bengali music, poetry, and voice — folk and traditional composition, Rabindra Sangeet, Nazrul Sangeet, voice-over, and dubbing — at Mohikontok Sound Lab, 1389 Kearney Ave, Bronx, NY 10465, serving the Bengali community across New York City.

What we record

Folk & Traditional Composition

Bengali folk and traditional songs, recorded in a treated room — the tradition the Lab's own catalog is built on.

Rabindra & Nazrul Sangeet

Tagore and Nazrul songs, recorded with the care their poetry deserves, by singers and musicians working with human hands and modern technology.

Voice-Over & Dubbing

Bengali voice-over and dubbing, including English-to-Bengali dubbing that carries a voice across languages — as in the Lab's Bengali dub of Franz Kafka's The Metamorphosis.

A listen-first archive, growing here

The Lab is building a catalog of Bangla content — music, poetry, drama, film dubs, and community recordings — made to be heard, so that community members who cannot read but can listen can still know the literature. Recording at the Lab adds to that living archive, held and cataloged from the Bronx studio.
