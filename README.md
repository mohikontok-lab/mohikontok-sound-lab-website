# Mohikontok Sound Lab website

Static rebuild of the Mohikontok Sound Lab studio site (mohikontoksoundlab.com),
built November 1, 2026 from the live site's captured content: Home, Venue, and
Contact pages, including the booking form and rate calculator.

This is fresh code written to match the live site's content — not a byte-for-byte
copy of the original HTML, which lives inside a Google Sites embed and could not
be extracted automatically. Contact details on these pages are the ones shown in
the live site's footer. Photos and the Venue banner carousel were not carried
over (no image files were extracted).

Blog: blog.html is the index, with posts under posts/.

Venue photos: assets/photos/venue-1.jpg … venue-9.jpg, shown in the Venue page slideshow.

Music page (2026-11-02): music.html carries 12 Spotify embeds — mOhiKonTok Top Tracks (10) plus Fly over My Friend and Holud Re Tumi Kon Kon Kaje Lago — with a Music link in the nav on every page.

Logo + slideshow slide 10 (2026-11-02): assets/logo.jpg (mountain/waves mOHiKonTok mark) is the header logo on every page; the Sound Lab banner artwork is slide 10 of the Venue slideshow (assets/photos/venue-10.jpg).
